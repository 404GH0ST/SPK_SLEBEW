# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Admin koperasi is the primary user. The wider workflow also supports pakar or kepala koperasi for SWARA weighting, petugas lapangan for nasabah data and criteria scores, and pimpinan koperasi for reviewing ranking outcomes and exported reports.

Users work in an operational cooperative setting where loan eligibility decisions need to be entered, weighted, calculated, reviewed, and documented without ambiguity. They need the interface to make role permissions, data completeness, method steps, and ranking results easy to trust.

## Product Purpose

This product is a cooperative loan eligibility decision support system. It manages criteria, nasabah alternatives, criterion scores, SWARA weights, MARCOS calculations, ranking results, and PDF or Excel reporting.

Success means an admin koperasi can maintain reliable decision data, run or review SWARA-MARCOS calculations, and explain why a nasabah is ranked as eligible, considered, or not eligible.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, operational.

The interface should feel like a serious internal decision tool: clear enough for repeated data entry, exact enough for academic-method transparency, and calm enough for cooperative staff making financial eligibility decisions.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy fintech styling, marketing-heavy dashboard patterns, unclear academic-method screens, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, and layouts that solve every section with another card.

The authenticated app should not feel like a landing page, analytics toy, or generic SaaS template. It should prioritize data clarity, workflow order, and traceable calculations.

For the public landing page only, the design may use an overdrive direction: more ambitious layout, motion, and visual presence are allowed, as long as it avoids gradients and glassmorphism and still makes the SPK SWARA-MARCOS purpose immediately clear.

## Design Principles

1. Make the decision path traceable: each screen should show how data moves from criteria, to weights, to scores, to ranking.
2. Treat role boundaries as part of the interface: admin, pakar, petugas, and pimpinan should always understand what they can act on.
3. Keep operational screens dense but readable: tables, forms, and status indicators should support repeated work without visual noise.
4. Explain methods at the point of use: SWARA and MARCOS details should help users act correctly, not become decorative textbook copy.
5. Let the landing page carry energy, while the app carries trust: public presentation can be more expressive; authenticated workflows should stay disciplined.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible workflows. Use readable type sizes for table-heavy screens, visible focus states, clear form errors, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated elements.

Avoid relying on color alone for eligibility, validation, or role status; pair color with text labels or icons.
